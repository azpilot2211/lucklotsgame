Lucky Lots: Card City — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Lucky Lots: Card City ("the game") is developed and published by Michael Driggs ("we", "us"). This policy describes what information the game handles.

The short version

You can play without an account. Signing in is optional, and so is every ad — you only ever see one by choosing to watch it for a reward. Gameplay analytics and crash diagnostics are switched off unless you turn them on.

If you sign in, the game has online features: a shared player roster and a team chat room. Anything you type in chat is sent to our server and shown to other players. Guest and signed-out play sends nothing off your device.

Data the game stores on your device

  • Save data — your game progress (houses, cards, tokens, rivals), stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Local play statistics — anonymous counters (e.g. how many card deals or building stages you played per day), stored in the same private storage. These never leave your device and contain nothing that identifies you.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of the above.

Optional account data

You can play as a guest. If you choose to sign in, Firebase Authentication processes an account identifier, and the game may receive the display name and profile photo on the Google account you linked. Account linking is optional and the game is fully playable without it.

Online play and team chat

These features run only when you are signed in. Guest and offline play uses none of them and sends nothing to our server.

  • Player roster — so other players can find you as a rival, the game publishes your display name, your city name, your property value, your loose token count, and whether your shield and guard dog are active. This is gameplay state, not personal information, and it is replaced each time it updates.
  • Team chat — messages you send go to our server so other players can read them. Each message stores the text (up to 200 characters), the display name you chose, an account identifier, and the time it was sent. Anything you type in chat is public to other players — please do not put personal information in it. Messages are deleted automatically after seven days.

Chat is for talking to other players about the game. We may remove messages and restrict accounts that use it to harass others or to post unlawful content.

Optional rewarded ads

The game shows no ads on its own. When you choose Watch ad in exchange for a reward, Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform may process information needed to request, deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and apply consent choices to that ad. Depending on your device and region this can include IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and your consent choices. Closing an ad before it finishes gives no reward.

Google describes its own practices in the Google Privacy Policy and its Google Mobile Ads SDK data-disclosure guidance.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

The Settings privacy panel has separate controls for anonymous gameplay analytics and for crash diagnostics. Both are off until you choose to turn them on, and you can change them later. When enabled, Firebase Analytics may receive gameplay event and counter data, and Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash and diagnostic information, including the device and app details needed to work out what failed.

This website (waitlist signup)

Everything above describes the game app. The website at luckylotsgame.com is separate:

  • Email waitlist — if you enter your email to be notified about testing, we store it only to send you the test invite and occasional updates about it. We will never sell or share it, and every email includes a way to opt out. Ask us to remove you anytime at azpilot@gmail.com.
  • Meta Pixel — the website uses the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure how our ads and posts perform. You can limit this in your Facebook ad settings or with a tracker-blocking browser/extension.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only for optional local reminders (e.g. your free deals are ready). These are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can decline the permission or disable notifications at any time in system settings, and the game works fully without them.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. It contains no real-money wagering. It does contain a team chat room where players type free text to each other, which is why it is not directed to children. Rewarded ads are optional and use a maximum ad-content rating of PG.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device.

To delete the data held on our server — your roster profile and your chat messages — email azpilot@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or include the Account ID shown in the game's Settings screen. We delete it within 30 days. Chat messages are also deleted automatically after seven days whether or not you ask.

Changes

If a future update changes what data the game handles, this policy will be updated and the change noted in the update's store notes before it takes effect.
