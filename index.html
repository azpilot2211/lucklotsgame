Good to know

Questions before your first deal?

Is Lucky Lots free to play?

Yes. Lucky Lots is free to download and play on Android, with optional in-app purchases.

Where can I download Lucky Lots?

Lucky Lots: Card City Builder is live on Google Play.

What are Tickets, Tokens, and Chips?

Tickets pay for Card Table deals, Tokens pay game prices such as building and repairs, and Chips pay for the Bonus Wheel and mini-games.

Are rewarded ads required?

No. Rewarded ads are optional: watch one when you want the offered reward.

Can I play in short sessions?

Yes. Deal a few hands, move a house forward, collect rewards, and return when Tickets and rent have recovered.

How does saving work?

Progress saves locally first. Cloud sync is available only when the build, service, sign-in, and connection support it, so an unavailable cloud status is not cloud protection.

Where can I read the full rules?

Open the How to Play guide for exact rules covering cards, construction, rivals, bonus games, Cities, events, purchases, saves, and safety.
