# First 30 days

Launch: verify Vercel build, canonical host, HTTPS, sitemap, robots, app-ads.txt, legal pages, Play links, analytics event receipt, and Android handoff. Submit the sitemap in Search Console and inspect the homepage plus one guide URL.

- Week 1: watch deploy errors, indexed URLs, Play clicks, landing-page engagement, and support feedback; fix only defects.
- Week 2: review Search Console queries and Play Console acquisition; begin hero copy test if traffic can support it.
- Week 3: review guide entrances and internal paths; run the winning hero against the screenshot test.
- Week 4: consolidate results, publish only verified news, and decide whether the CTA-placement test has enough traffic.

Use owned social posts and game-community announcements to link to the most relevant guide, not only the homepage. At low volume, track crawl/index coverage, Play-click rate, placement mix, broken links, and support-question themes. Stop changing a variant until it has run at least two full weeks and has enough eligible sessions for a directional result, unless it causes a verified defect.
