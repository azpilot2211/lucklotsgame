Verdict: ready to launch. The mobile first viewport identifies Lucky Lots, explains the card-to-building promise, shows authentic game art, and presents the Google Play action. Organic guide and news visitors receive crawlable factual content, nearby related links, and a Play action without blocking interstitials.

The highest-impact fixes are present: the promise and CTA fit at 320x568; all 12 authoritative rule topics are mapped into six linked guides; and live metadata, canonicals, structured data, robots, sitemap, and stale-claim checks are automated.

Run tests sequentially: (1) current hero headline versus a shorter building-led variant, (2) neighborhood transformation art versus a gameplay screenshot, then (3) guide mid-content Play CTA versus end-only. The primary metric is `play_store_click` events divided by eligible landing sessions, segmented by placement and landing page. An outbound click is not a confirmed install.
